PROJECT EVALUATION & ASSESSMENT SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

Projects are assessed using a comprehensive 1000-point rubric covering seven dimensions: 
Problem Understanding, Solution Quality, Research & Analysis, Presentation & Clarity, 
Innovation & Creativity, Feasibility & Practicality and CV alignment. Assessment is 
conducted through a combination of manual review and AI-powered evaluation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DIMENSION 1: PROBLEM UNDERSTANDING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Domain & Product Brief: RiskLens Technologies is a Series B fintech startup ($25M funding) building a next-generation institutional risk monitoring platform to compete with Bloomberg PORT, MSCI RiskMetrics, and Axioma.
- Core Challenge Addressed: Transformed crude wireframe concepts into a world-class, production-grade UI/UX design system balancing ultra-dense financial data, sub-second latency look-and-feel, dynamic persona customization, and 100% WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility.
- Target Persona Multiplicity: Fully catered to 4 distinct user roles with fundamentally different analytical needs:
  1. Chief Risk Officer (CRO): Executive oversight, capital adequacy, macro stress testing, board presentation.
  2. Senior Portfolio Manager (PM): Risk budget utilization, active VaR, sector treemaps, "What-If" trade sandbox.
  3. Junior Risk Analyst: Option Greeks (Delta, Gamma, Vega, Theta, Rho), Monte Carlo simulations, volatility skew.
  4. Compliance Officer: Regulatory breach matrix (Basel III/IV, FRTB), timestamp-accurate audit trail logs.
- Multi-Device Strategy: Solved data-density degradation across 3 device breakpoints (Desktop 1440px, Tablet 768px, Mobile 375px) via adaptive information architecture rather than naive pinch-to-zoom reflowing.

  4. Compliance & Regulatory Breach Matrix Screen
  5. Monte Carlo VaR Simulation Engine Screen

  8. Live Incident Alert Center Screen
  9. "What-If" Trade & Macro Shock Sandbox Screen
  10. Liquidity & Counterparty Risk Screen
  11. Immutable Audit Trail & Governance Log Screen
  12. Design System & Handoff Specification Screen
- Design Token Grid: Built on an 8px spatial grid foundation with designated Bloomberg high-density modes (Compact 32px, Standard 40px, Touch 48px).

DIMENSION 3: RESEARCH & ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Quantitative Financial Analytics: Accurately modeled complex financial metrics:
  - 1-Day 95% Value-at-Risk (VaR) & 1-Day 99% Conditional VaR (Expected Shortfall / CVaR).
  - First & Second-order Option Greeks (Delta, Gamma, Vega, Theta, Rho).
  - Historical Macro Stress Scenarios (2008 Lehman GFC Replay, COVID March 2020 Crash, Rate Shift +200bps).
  - Peak-to-Trough Drawdown Waterfall Chart & NxN Pearson Asset Correlation Matrix.
  - Barra Factor Attribution (Momentum, Value, Volatility, Size, Growth, Quality).
